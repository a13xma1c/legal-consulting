About {{ site_name }}

We believe good legal help should be understandable, accessible and fairly priced.

{{ site_name }} was founded by lawyers who were frustrated that so many people only sought advice once a problem had become a crisis. Our platform combines experienced practitioners with modern tools - a searchable library of plain-English articles, a moderated community forum and an AI assistant that can help you understand your situation before you ever speak to a lawyer.

Our principles

  • Plain English. If you can't understand the advice, it isn't useful.
  • Transparent costs. You'll always know what something will cost before you commit.
  • Early intervention. Most legal problems are cheaper and easier to fix early.
